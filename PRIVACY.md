# Professional-profile privacy policy

This project publishes public professional information relevant to private-equity research: names, business roles, firm affiliations, disclosed transactions, boards, professional writing and office geography.

It does not intentionally publish personal email addresses, private telephone numbers, home addresses, family details, protected characteristics, login credentials or inferred personal attributes. Transaction documents sometimes contain business contact details; those details are omitted from the dataset.

When a submitted professional directory contains contact addresses, the import process discards them before generating canonical records, public downloads or profile pages. Supplied professional profile links may be retained and are labeled as submitted rather than independently verified.

Profiles are not lead-generation dossiers. A person may request correction of an inaccurate professional fact through the repository correction process.
