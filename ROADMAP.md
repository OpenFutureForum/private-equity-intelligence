# Roadmap

## 0.2: National coverage foundation (completed)

- Launch a cited atlas across 10 major U.S. private-equity hubs.
- Expand current professional and firm coverage beyond the San Francisco Bay Area.
- Add transaction-level debt, legal and diligence relationships when publicly disclosed.
- Add a visual national map, market pages and market data exports.

## 0.3: National depth

- Reach 25 relationship-economy transactions.
- Add Atlanta, Miami, Minneapolis, Philadelphia, Denver and additional Texas coverage.
- Deepen each existing market with sector, strategy and seniority coverage.
- Add provider practice leaders only when official biographies establish PE responsibility.

## 0.5: Intelligence layer

- Publish evidence-coverage scores.
- Add time-aware ownership chains and add-on histories.
- Add sector, strategy, geography and deal-year comparisons.
- Publish the first Open Future Forum PE survey with question wording and denominators.

## 1.0: Citable annual edition

- Publish a reviewed annual research report.
- Release versioned data exports and a stable API contract.
- Register preservation only after release metadata and integrations are verified.
- Consider rankings only after eligibility and evidence thresholds are public and tested.
