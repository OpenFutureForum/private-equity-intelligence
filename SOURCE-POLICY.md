# Source policy

## Requirements

Every material public claim must trace to a canonical source record. Source records contain the URL, title, publisher, source class, publication date when available, access date and supported claims.

## Preferred sources

Regulatory filings and official transaction documents are preferred for transaction mechanics. Official firm biographies and directories support current titles, team classifications and published office locations. Adviser announcements support that adviser’s own role and named team, but do not independently establish unrelated transaction terms.

Official portfolio directories may establish that a firm identifies a company as an investment, along with the directory's investment year, asset class, industry and headquarters fields. They do not establish an announcement date, transaction value, seller, advisers or individual deal-team participation. Records based on a portfolio directory are labeled `official_portfolio_record` and keep those fields blank.

## User-supplied professional directories

Submitted professional lists are a separate source class. Their names, organizations, titles, tiers and professional profile links may be reproduced when the submission is authorized for publication, but every record remains labeled `user_supplied_unverified` until independently checked. These entries do not establish a person's location, current employment, deal participation or endorsement. Duplicate rows are merged. Personal contact addresses are removed before publication.

## Licensed databases

PitchBook, Preqin, Capital IQ, Crunchbase, LinkedIn and other subscription or platform data may help discovery when lawful, but their licensed content is not republished. Public claims require public, reusable evidence.

## Conflicts

Conflicting credible sources are retained and described rather than silently reconciled. Unknown or undisclosed values are not estimated unless a transparent derivation is published.
