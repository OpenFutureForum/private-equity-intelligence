# Source policy

## Requirements

Every material public claim must trace to a canonical source record. Source records contain the URL, title, publisher, source class, publication date when available, access date and supported claims.

## Preferred sources

Regulatory filings and official transaction documents are preferred for transaction mechanics. Official firm biographies support current titles and disclosed responsibilities. Adviser announcements support that adviser’s own role and named team, but do not independently establish unrelated transaction terms.

## Licensed databases

PitchBook, Preqin, Capital IQ, Crunchbase, LinkedIn and other subscription or platform data may help discovery when lawful, but their licensed content is not republished. Public claims require public, reusable evidence.

## Conflicts

Conflicting credible sources are retained and described rather than silently reconciled. Unknown or undisclosed values are not estimated unless a transparent derivation is published.
