# Third-party notices

## U.S. map geometry

The United States atlas uses `us-atlas`, version 3, to generate map geometry at build time.

`us-atlas` provides TopoJSON derived from U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary shapefiles. The package is distributed under the ISC License. Copyright 2013-2019 Michael Bostock.

- Source: https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas
- License: https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas/blob/master/LICENSE
- Census cartographic boundaries: https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html
