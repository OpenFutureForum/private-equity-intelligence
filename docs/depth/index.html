The standard

Depth means complete, bounded evidence.

Every record must connect a sourced company identity, ownership, chronology, transaction classification, company facts and review status. Announcement records add disclosed advisers, lenders, named professionals, value and research findings. Portfolio records do not lose credit for facts their source does not disclose. Those gaps remain explicit. The completion threshold remains 75.
