Purpose and scope

The index maps public, professionally relevant evidence about private-equity transactions, firms, people and advisers. The National Edition adds a ten-market United States atlas while retaining a concentrated transaction cohort in executive networks, professional communities, B2B events and private-markets information.

01

Start with primary evidence

Regulatory records, transaction documents, company announcements and official biographies carry the most weight.

02

Attribute people narrowly

A person is connected to a deal only when a source names or quotes them in that transaction context.

03

Separate current and historical

Transaction roles are historical. Current titles and locations require current first-party evidence.

04

Expose uncertainty

Unknown, undisclosed and not-yet-verified are not converted into estimates or implications.

Confirmed person-to-deal labels

  • named_deal_team — directly identified as part of a transaction workstream.
  • named_adviser — named by an adviser or transaction document.
  • official_transaction_spokesperson — quoted or listed as a responsible firm representative.

United States atlas location evidence

explicit_person_bio is the strongest location mode. explicit_office_filter means the person is returned by an official firm's location filter or office directory. firm_office_association is visibly weaker: the firm has a verified office in the market, but the source does not establish the person's own work location. A deal, headquarters or firm membership alone never becomes a personal-location claim.

National coverage boundaries

The ten hubs are curated entry points, not an exhaustive census of U.S. private equity. A market appears when source-backed people or firm-presence records support it. Missing people, firms and cities remain not-yet-verified rather than being treated as absent from the real-world market.

Scoring

The relationship-economy dimensions are published as questions and evidence prompts. No company scores or rankings appear until evidence thresholds, weighting and review procedures have been defined and tested.

Corrections and reproducibility

Each canonical record carries a verification date and source references. The generated website and downloads are built deterministically from those records. Corrections should identify the exact claim and a reliable replacement source.
