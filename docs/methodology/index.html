Research standard

Methodology

The rules for evidence, attribution, freshness and privacy used throughout the index.

Source First

Undisclosed facts are left blank.

Purpose and scope

The index maps public, professionally relevant evidence about private-equity transactions, firms, people and advisers. The National Edition adds a ten-market United States atlas and retains a focused transaction set covering executive networks, professional communities, B2B events and private-markets information.

01

Start with primary evidence

Regulatory records, transaction documents, company announcements and official biographies carry the most weight.

02

Attribute people narrowly

A person is connected to a deal only when a source names or quotes them in that transaction context.

03

Separate current and historical

Transaction roles are historical. Current titles and locations require current first-party evidence.

04

State uncertainty

Unknown, undisclosed and unverified facts are not turned into estimates.

Confirmed person-to-deal labels

  • named_deal_team: directly identified as part of a transaction workstream.
  • named_adviser: named by an adviser or transaction document.
  • official_transaction_spokesperson: quoted or listed as a responsible firm representative.

United States atlas location evidence

explicit_person_bio is the strongest location mode. explicit_office_filter means an official firm directory returns the person for that office. firm_office_association means the firm has a verified office in the market, but the source does not establish the person's own work location. A transaction, headquarters address or firm membership does not establish personal location.

National coverage boundaries

The ten hubs are selected research markets. They are not a census of U.S. private equity. A market appears when public sources support its people or firm-presence records. Missing people, firms and cities have not been verified.

Scoring

The relationship-economy dimensions are published as questions and evidence prompts. Company scores and rankings will require published evidence thresholds, weights and review procedures.

Corrections and reproducibility

Each canonical record carries a verification date and source references. The website and downloads are generated from those records. Corrections should identify the exact claim and a reliable replacement source.
