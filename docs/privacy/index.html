Professional-profile standard

Privacy

A narrow public-interest profile policy for professionally relevant private-equity research.

Included

Public names, business roles, firm affiliations, disclosed transactions, professional boards and office geography that directly support the research purpose.

Excluded

Personal email addresses, private telephone numbers, home addresses, family details, protected characteristics, credentials and inferred personal attributes. Transaction documents may contain business contact details; this dataset omits them.

Not a lead-generation dossier

Profiles are evidence records, not sales dossiers. A person may request correction of an inaccurate professional fact through the repository process.
