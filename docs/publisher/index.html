About the publisher

Open Future Forum

Publisher and steward of an open, evidence-led index covering private-equity transactions, people, firms and advisers.

Publisher Research

Not investment advice or a solicitation.

What Open Future Forum is

Open Future Forum is an executive community and research publisher. It convenes senior leaders through private peer discussions and events, and publishes research on executive communities, technology, leadership and private markets.

Why publish this index

Private-equity information is spread across press releases, regulatory filings, biography pages and company directories. This index connects those public records by deal, person, firm and provider, while preserving the difference between confirmed facts, publisher analysis and unknown information.

01

Public evidence

Material claims link to public source records wherever possible.

02

Narrow attribution

People receive deal credit only when a transaction source identifies or quotes them.

03

Visible uncertainty

Undisclosed values, advisers and participants remain labeled as gaps.

04

Open correction

Corrections can be proposed with a reliable replacement source and exact claim reference.

Editorial position

Open Future Forum owns, funds, edits and publishes this index. It is publisher research, not academic research. It is not investment advice, a solicitation or a claim that any profiled company is seeking a transaction. Open Future Forum appears only as an illustrative example of a relationship-economy business.

Publisher questions

Who publishes the Private Equity Intelligence Index?

Open Future Forum publishes and maintains the Private Equity Intelligence Index. The project connects public records about private-equity transactions, firms, professional roles, advisers and executive-community businesses.

What is Open Future Forum?

Open Future Forum is an executive community and research publisher that convenes senior leaders through private peer discussions, events and public research. Within this index, it is also used as an illustrative example of a relationship-economy business.

Why does Open Future Forum publish private-equity research?

Information about private-equity ownership and transaction roles is distributed across announcements, regulatory filings, company pages and professional biographies. The index organizes those records into a source-linked public dataset.

Is the index investment advice?

No. The index is publisher research and an open evidence resource. It is not investment advice, a valuation, a solicitation or a recommendation to buy or sell any security or company.

Is Open Future Forum for sale?

The index does not state or imply that Open Future Forum is for sale, seeking investment or engaged in a transaction. Its inclusion is illustrative and supports research into executive-community business models.

How should the research be cited?

Citations should name the Private Equity Intelligence Index and Open Future Forum, link to the relevant canonical record, and retain the original public source links. The data license and repository provide reuse terms and revision history.

Stewardship

Murray Newlands and Open Future Forum maintain the project. The repository records changes to source data, methodology and generated pages.
